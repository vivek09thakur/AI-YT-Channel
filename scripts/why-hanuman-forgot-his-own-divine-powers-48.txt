What happens when the strongest being in the universe forgets his own strength...

As a child, young Hanuman flies toward the sun, mistaking it for a ripe golden fruit. The gods panic, and Indra hurls his thunderbolt, striking the little monkey down.

Vayu, the wind god, is heartbroken. He draws all air from the three worlds, bringing creation to the brink of suffocation. To restore balance, Brahma and the gods shower Hanuman with ultimate boons of invincibility.

Yet, a group of playful sages worry about his endless pranks. They place a gentle curse upon him... he will completely forget his immense powers until someone reminds him in his hour of greatest need.

Years later, sitting quietly on a shore, he needs this exact memory to leap across the ocean.

Sometimes, your greatest strength only awakens when you finally remember who you truly are.
