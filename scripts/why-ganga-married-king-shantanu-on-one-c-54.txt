Would you marry someone who begs you never to question their actions... no matter how cruel they seem? 

King Shantanu falls hopelessly in love with a breathtaking celestial woman by the riverbank. She agrees to be his queen, but drops a chilling ultimatum: he must never ask why she does what she does, or she will vanish forever.

The king agrees, blinded by desire. Yet his heart shatters into pieces when his divine bride calmly walks to the rushing river... and drowns their first newborn child.

He stays silent, bound by his promise, as she repeats this unthinkable act seven more times. 

When she lifts their eighth son toward the water, the broken father finally breaks his vow and screams why. 

She smiles, holding the crying baby alive, and reveals the shocking truth. These weren't murders, but a merciful release from a heavy curse for celestial beings.

She vanishes into the mist, leaving a grieving king and a child destined to shape the Mahabharata.
