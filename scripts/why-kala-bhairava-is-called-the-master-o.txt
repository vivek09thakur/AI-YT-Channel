What if time itself had a master? /  In Hindu tradition, Kala Bhairava is worshipped as the Lord of Time… the divine force beyond birth, death, and destruction. /  But why is Bhairava given this extraordinary title? /  The word Kala carries a powerful meaning. /  It represents time… and it also represents death. /  Everything in this universe is controlled by time. /  Kings rise… empires fall… stars are born… and entire worlds eventually disappear. /  Nothing can escape its passage. /  But Kala Bhairava represents the divine power that stands beyond these limitations. /  He reminds us that time waits for no one… and that every action has its consequences. /  In his fierce form, Bhairava destroys arrogance, ignorance, and the illusion that life will last forever. /  He is also worshipped as the guardian of Kashi… the sacred city where devotees seek liberation from the cycle of birth and death. /  His presence teaches a profound lesson. /  Do not waste the time you have been given. /  Use it to follow dharma… overcome your ego… and move closer to the truth. /  For everything in this universe is temporary. /  But the eternal reality beyond time… can never be destroyed. /  That is why devotees remember him as Kala Bhairava… the Lord who governs time and reminds all beings of their ultimate destiny. /  But how did this terrifying form of Lord Shiva become the protector of devotees? /  And why is a dog always seen beside him? /  That is the mystery we will uncover in the next episode.
