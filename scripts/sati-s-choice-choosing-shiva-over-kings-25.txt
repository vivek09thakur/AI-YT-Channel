The palace of Daksha echoes with whispers of discontent... and the clinking of royal gold.

Kings and princes from every corner of the earth have gathered. They come adorned in rubies and silks. They seek the hand of the proud emperor's radiant daughter.

Sati walks among them like a silent flame. Her eyes do not linger on the glittering crowns. Her heart is already wandering the distant, lonely peaks of Mount Kailash.

Her father points to the mightiest king of all. Sati simply shakes her head. The court gasps in absolute shock.

Daksha's face turns the color of bruised ash. How can she reject such unmatched worldly wealth? What king could possibly rival these sovereigns?

Sati lifts her chin with quiet defiance. She speaks no words of standard courtly praise. She only holds out an empty palm... waiting for an unseen ash-smear.

In her mind, the grand thrones dissolve into mist. She sees only wild matted locks and a tiger skin. She hears only the beat of a distant damru.

True royalty wears no gold, she whispers to the silent hall. True power sits in meditation upon the snow.

She leaves the assembly hall before her father can roar. Her bare feet touch the marble floor like falling petals.

The path to the ascetic lord is long and steep. But Sati has already made her eternal choice...

And the mountains are already waiting.
