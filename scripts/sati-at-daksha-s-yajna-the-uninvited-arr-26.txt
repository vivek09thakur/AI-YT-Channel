A grand canopy of white silk rises proudly above the vast sacrificial grounds... Drums beat a rhythmic pulse of immense royal pride, echoing far across the glittering capital of King Daksha.

From the high and silent mountains, a quiet longing suddenly stirs. Sati hears faint whispers of a great gathering of all her kin... and her gentle heart reaches out for the distant home she left behind.

She speaks her heartfelt wish to the blue-skinned lord of Kailash. Shiva slowly closes his dark, knowing eyes, sensing the heavy shadow of fate... and gently warns her of the bitter cold that always awaits uninvited guests.

Undeterred, Sati steps through the towering golden gates completely alone. The festive music falters and dies. The assembled priests turn away in shame, their eyes sharp with quiet judgment and deep unspoken contempt.

King Daksha stands tall before the crackling sacred fire. He sees his youngest daughter standing there, wrapped in simple mountain ash and dust... and his royal face darkens rapidly like a furious monsoon storm.

He raises his booming voice to pierce the heavy silence of the hall. He mocks the wild ascetic husband who sits in lonely cremation grounds, openly calling him a vagrant wanderer entirely unworthy of respect.

Sati feels the blistering heat of the ritual fire, but an infinitely deeper fire awakens violently within her chest. The fierce blood of the primordial mother boils instantly at the open insult to her beloved lord.

She stands entirely tall against the gathering storm of her father's unchecked wrath. Every wide eye in the grand assembly turns to watch the unfolding tragedy of a broken family.

Royal pride shatters completely under the crushing weight of blinding arrogance. The stage is finally set for the ultimate sacrifice of the heart, and the terrifying fury of the awakening cosmos.
