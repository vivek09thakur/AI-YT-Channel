Goddess Parvati sits alone in her quiet chamber on Mount Kailash... her mind busy with thoughts of absolute loyalty and protection.

She takes turmeric paste and sacred oils from her own skin... and molds them with her own hands into the form of a handsome boy.

She breathes life into the clay figure... and a radiant young prince opens his eyes, bowing deeply to his divine mother.

Parvati smiles with fierce maternal love... and appoints him as her personal guardian, commanding him to let no one pass while she bathes.

Soon, Lord Shiva himself arrives at the gates... his majestic trident gleaming, his matted locks swaying with the cosmic wind.

The divine boy steps forward, blocking the path with quiet courage... refusing entry to the mighty destroyer of the universe.

Shiva is stunned by the mortal child's audacity... and his divine attendants rush forward to clear the path, but the boy defeats them all with ease.

Enraged by this unknown challenger, Shiva draws his shiny blade... and in a sudden flash of cosmic wrath, strikes off the boy's head.

Goddess Parvati rushes out from her chambers, screaming in unbearable grief... as she beholds her lifeless creation lying in a pool of blood.

Realizing his terrible mistake, the Lord of Kailash vows to restore life to the brave child... setting in motion a divine quest that will shake the three worlds.
