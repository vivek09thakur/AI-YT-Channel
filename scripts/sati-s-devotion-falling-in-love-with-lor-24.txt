A majestic royal palace echoes with the silent, unwavering prayers of a young princess who refuses to look at anyone else.

Princess Sati closes her eyes and paints the luminous image of the great ascetic in her mind. Her proud father Daksha wants a powerful warrior prince for her throne. Sati wants only the lord of the high mountains.

She leaves the soft comforts of silk and gold far behind. She walks deep into the silent, ancient forest where the wild winds whisper only one sacred name. Shiva...

Days turn into long, unforgiving seasons of hardship. Sati eats only dry fallen leaves, then only drops of morning dew, and finally nothing at all. Her fierce penance burns like a pure flame against the dark woods.

Wild beasts gather quietly around her, sensing her immense and growing spiritual power. Fierce tigers sit as gentle guards. Venomous snakes bow their hoods in deep respect to her unbreakable focus.

Finally, the solid earth trembles ever so slightly beneath the trees. A cool, fragrant breeze sweeps through the canopy carrying the deep scent of sacred ash and Himalayan snow.

Lord Shiva suddenly appears before her, draped in tiger skin, His matted locks holding the bright crescent moon. He smiles gently at the devoted princess who dared to conquer the cosmos with pure love.

Their divine union will soon bridge two entirely different worlds, setting the stage for a great cosmic love story that transcends time itself.
