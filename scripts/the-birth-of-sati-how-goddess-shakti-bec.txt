Before Goddess Parvati was born… there was Sati.  And her birth was destined to change the life of Lord Shiva forever. /  Daksha Prajapati, the son of Brahma, desired to have the divine Goddess Shakti as his daughter. /  He performed intense penance… praying for the Goddess to bless his family. /  Pleased by his devotion, the divine Mother appeared before him. /  Daksha bowed and requested her to be born as his daughter… so that she could become the wife of Lord Shiva. /  The Goddess accepted his wish… but gave him a warning.  She told Daksha that if he ever disrespected her… or forgot her divine nature… she would abandon that body and return to her eternal form. /  Soon, the Goddess was born into Daksha's family… as Sati, the daughter of Daksha and Asikni. /  Her birth brought joy to the family… but her destiny was far greater than anyone could imagine. /  From childhood, Sati felt a deep connection with Lord Shiva… the supreme yogi who lived beyond worldly desires. /  Her heart was devoted to him alone. /  But her father, Daksha, would one day develop a deep hatred toward Shiva. /  And the very daughter he had prayed for… would become the reason for a conflict that shook the heavens. /  This was the beginning of Sati's story… a story of divine love, devotion, pride, and tragedy. /  In the next episode… Sati begins her penance to win Lord Shiva as her husband.
