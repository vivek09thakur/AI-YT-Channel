Imagine the greatest warrior on the battlefield... helpless because of a forgotten curse. 

Karna raises his mighty bow, aiming at Arjuna. Victory is finally within his grasp. Suddenly, the earth groans beneath him. 

The heavy wheel of Karna's chariot sinks deep into the wet mud. Desperate, he steps down to lift it with his bare hands. 

Arjuna hesitates, but Lord Krishna urges him forward. Remember the innocent cow Karna once struck by accident, and the sorrowful curse the grieving Brahmin laid upon him? 

Now, in the exact moment he drops his weapons to save his chariot, karma catches up. 

An arrow flies through the dust. The unconquerable hero falls. 

Not by superior skill... but by the unyielding weight of past mistakes.
