Can a giant bird defeat an almighty demon flying in a golden chariot...?

High in the burning sky, brave Jatayu spots Ravana fleeing with weeping Sita. He cannot look away. This noble vulture remembers a promise made to King Dasharatha. He vows to protect the house of Raghu with his final breath.

With a mighty roar, Jatayu attacks. His razor-sharp talons shatter Ravana's golden chariot. Splinters rain down like autumn leaves. The demon king staggers, shocked by the sheer fury of an aged bird.

Enraged, Ravana draws his celestial sword. Jatayu fights on, bleeding and battered, trading flesh and feathers for Sita's freedom. But time and the demon's dark boons are too strong. With one merciless strike, the sacred sword severs Jatayu's mighty wing.

He plummets from the heavens, crashing onto the burning earth. Yet, as Sita weeps for her fallen protector, Jatayu stays conscious just long enough... to tell Rama the truth.

Even in certain defeat, a hero's duty is his ultimate weapon.
