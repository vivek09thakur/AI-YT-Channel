What is the true price of ambition?

Young Ekalavya bows before a clay statue of Dronacharya, mastering archery in total solitude. Through sheer willpower, he surpasses even the greatest princes of Hastinapura in skill.

One day, Drona and his royal students discover the forest archer in action. A barking hound interrupts Ekalavya's focus, and with a blur of arrows, he seals the dog's mouth without shedding a single drop of blood.

Stunned by this impossible talent, Drona demands a terrible fee for his unspoken mentorship. He asks for Ekalavya's right thumb.

Without a moment of hesitation, the loyal hunter draws his knife and severs it, offering it to the master.

Skill without a guru is dangerous, but genius without a thumb changes the history of war forever.
