The ashes of grief still linger in the silent cosmos... but a fierce new fire is quietly burning deep within the snow-capped Himalayas.

Princess Parvati sheds her heavy silks and royal ornaments. She wears rough tree bark and smears her skin with simple forest mud. She trades lavish royal feasts for the bitter taste of falling leaves and morning dew.

Far away, Lord Shiva sits deep in eternal meditation on his lonely snowy peak. His eyes are closed to the shifting illusions of the material world. His heart is locked behind an impenetrable wall of absolute detachment.

Undeterred, Parvati stands unwavering upon one foot amidst freezing winter gales. She raises her delicate hands to the frozen sky. She whispers only one sacred name into the howling wind... Shiva, Shiva, Shiva.

Then summer arrives with scorching, blistering heat across the land. Parvati sits calmly surrounded by four blazing sacrificial fires. She stares unflinchingly at the burning, ruthless sun. Her unrelenting penance shakes the heavens themselves.

The gods look down from the clouds in sheer awe. Sage Narada watches the fierce, unbroken devotion unfold with a gentle smile. Even the ancient mountains seem to bow in deep reverence to her unstoppable, iron will.

Day after day, month after month, year after year, the holy discipline holds firm. There are no complaints of fatigue or doubt. There is only love, pure and unyielding as solid rock.

Shiva finally opens his third eye at last. The cosmic ice begins to melt into rushing streams. A mighty tremor ripples through creation... because true, selfless devotion can awaken even the lord of silence.
