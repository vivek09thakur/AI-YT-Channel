Silence falls over the entire cosmos as the sacred flames finally consume the mortal form of Sati... leaving behind a void that no god can ever fill.

Lord Shiva arrives at the devastated yajna arena far too late to stop the unimaginable tragedy.

He moves through the ruined courtyard like a phantom, bypassing terrified priests and trembling kings.

He finds her lifeless body resting gently in his arms... cold, still, and completely devoid of breath.

Time freezes across the three worlds. The great god of destruction does not weep.

Instead, an ocean of uncontainable grief turns instantly into absolute, blinding, universe-shattering fury... a wrath never before witnessed by creation.

Shiva lifts Sati upon his mighty shoulder and begins the terrifying Tandava... the cosmic dance of destruction.

Every heavy stomp of his ash-covered foot shatters the tectonic foundations of the earth beneath him.

The heavens tremble in mortal terror. The stars fall from their celestial orbits like dying embers of a forgotten fire.

Fear grips every god in the pantheon as the entire universe spirals rapidly toward total annihilation.

Lord Vishnu steps forward bravely to intervene, casting his spinning discus to gracefully halt the catastrophic end of all things.

Sati's sacred body parts scatter across the geography of the earth... anchoring this eternal grief into holy sanctuaries.

Even in his immense, blinding sorrow, the seeds of divine rebirth are quietly planted across the sacred land.
