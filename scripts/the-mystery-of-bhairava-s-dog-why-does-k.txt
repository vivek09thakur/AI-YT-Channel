Have you ever wondered why a fierce form of Lord Shiva is always associated with a dog? /  Kala Bhairava, the mighty guardian of Kashi, is traditionally depicted with a dog as his divine companion and vehicle. /  But this is more than just an unusual symbol. /  In Hindu tradition, the dog represents loyalty, alertness, protection, and unwavering devotion. /  As Bhairava's companion, it reflects his role as the guardian who watches over the world and protects those who seek refuge in him. /  Bhairava is also known as the protector of sacred places and the destroyer of negative forces. /  His dog stands beside him as a symbol of vigilance… reminding us that divine protection is always watchful. /  In many traditions, devotees offer food to dogs as an act of reverence to Kala Bhairava. /  It is believed that serving these animals with kindness is one way of expressing devotion to the deity. /  The fierce Bhairava teaches us courage… while his loyal companion reminds us of humility and faithfulness. /  Together, they represent a powerful lesson: true strength is not only about defeating evil… it is also about protecting the vulnerable. /  That is why the image of Kala Bhairava with his dog continues to inspire devotion across generations. /  But Bhairava's mysteries do not end here. /  Why do devotees worship him on Ashtami… and what makes Kalashtami so sacred? /  That is the story of our next episode.
