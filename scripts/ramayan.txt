Before the beginning of time as we know it...
Before the greatest war ever witnessed...
There existed a world where gods walked among mortals, where destiny shaped kingdoms, and where the eternal battle between Dharma and Adharma was about to begin.

In the magnificent kingdom of Ayodhya...
A prince was born.
A prince whose name would echo through eternity.
Rama.
The son of Dasharatha.
The protector of the innocent.
The embodiment of righteousness.

But destiny does not always follow the path of happiness.
A crown was taken away.
A kingdom was left behind.
And a prince willingly embraced fourteen years of exile.

Alongside him walked Sita...
The embodiment of strength and devotion.
And Lakshmana...
Whose loyalty knew no boundaries.

Deep within the forests...
A darkness was awakening.
The mighty Ravana.
King of Lanka.
A conqueror feared by gods and mortals alike.
His pride would challenge the heavens.
His actions would ignite a war that would shake the foundations of the universe.

But he had underestimated one thing.
The power of Dharma.
The power of devotion.
And the strength of a promise.

From the forests of Panchavati...
To the shores of the mighty ocean...
From Hanuman's legendary leap...
To the burning skies of Lanka...
An army would rise.
A bridge would be built across the impossible.
And the greatest battle of all time would begin.

When darkness threatens the world...
When evil believes itself invincible...
When Dharma stands against Adharma...
One warrior will rise.
One arrow will change everything.
And one name...
Will become eternal.

RAMAYAN.
The story of a prince.
The journey of a god.
And the eternal victory of Dharma.
