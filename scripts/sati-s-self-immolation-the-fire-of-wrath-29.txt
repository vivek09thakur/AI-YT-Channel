The sacrificial arena falls dead silent. All eyes turn toward the uninvited princess...

Daksha stands tall on his elevated platform. His face contorts with blinding rage. He hurls insults at the ascetic lord of mountains. He mocks Shiva's matted locks, his ash-covered skin, his ghostly companions...

Sati listens. Every word is a poisoned dagger piercing her heart. She tries to defend her divine husband. She speaks of his infinite compassion, his cosmic grace, his boundless love...

Daksha refuses to listen. He turns his back on his own flesh and blood. He utters the ultimate blasphemy against the destroyer of worlds...

Something inside Sati breaks. The daughter of Daksha dies in that exact instant. Only the primordial Goddess remains. The supreme power of the universe wakes within a mortal form...

She steps forward. Her eyes burn with a terrifying inner light. She raises her hand and speaks a final, chilling truth. If a body born of a father's blood cannot honor the lord of all, this body must be cast away...

She sits in deep meditation. She closes her eyes and focuses her inner life-force. A mystical fire ignites from within her own soul. It blazes outward, fierce and pure, consuming her physical form in seconds...

Panic erupts among the gods. The sacred fire turns ash. The sacrifice is defiled. A grief so immense begins to take shape across the cosmos...

When a wife's honor is trampled, the universe itself burns. This is the tragic fire that awakens the wrath of Shiva.

From the ashes of devotion, the storm of vengeance is about to be born.
