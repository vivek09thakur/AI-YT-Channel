Did you know that Lord Shiva has a form in which he appears as a young child? This divine form is known as Batuk Bhairav.  Batuk Bhairav is considered the child form of Lord Bhairav, who is a fierce manifestation of Lord Shiva. He symbolizes protection, courage, strength, and divine blessings.  According to Hindu beliefs, Lord Shiva manifested as Bhairav to destroy evil and protect righteousness. Batuk Bhairav represents the gentler, childlike aspect of this divine energy.  But why do devotees worship Batuk Bhairav?  It is believed that worshipping him protects devotees from fear, negative energies, and obstacles in life. People pray to him for courage, peace, prosperity, and protection from unseen difficulties.  Devotees especially worship Batuk Bhairav on Sundays, Tuesdays, and Ashtami, considering these days auspicious.  Batuk Bhairav teaches us that divine protection is always with those who have faith, courage, and devotion.  Jai Batuk Bhairav!
