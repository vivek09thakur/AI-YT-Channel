Did you know that Lord Shiva's fierce form, Kala Bhairava, is believed to manifest in eight powerful forms to guard the eight directions? /  These divine manifestations are known as the Ashta Bhairavas. /  Each form represents a different aspect of divine power… and together, they protect sacred spaces from negative forces. /  The first is Asitanga Bhairava, the guardian of the East. /  The second is Ruru Bhairava, who protects the South-East. /  The third is Chanda Bhairava, the fierce guardian of the South. /  The fourth is Krodha Bhairava, who watches over the South-West. /  The fifth is Unmatta Bhairava, the guardian of the West. /  The sixth is Kapala Bhairava, who protects the North-West. /  The seventh is Bhishana Bhairava, the terrifying guardian of the North. /  And the eighth is Samhara Bhairava, the powerful force of destruction and transformation, who guards the North-East. /  Together, these eight forms create a divine circle of protection around sacred places, especially the holy city of Kashi. /  They are also associated with eight Mother Goddesses, known as the Ashta Matrikas. /  Although their names and arrangements differ across some Hindu traditions, their central purpose remains the same… to protect, destroy evil, and uphold divine order. /  The Ashta Bhairavas teach us that divine power has many forms… but its purpose is one. /  To protect righteousness and destroy ignorance. /  But among all these manifestations, one form holds a special place in the hearts of devotees. /  A gentle yet powerful form of Bhairava, worshipped by those seeking protection and freedom from fear. /  Who is Batuk Bhairava… and why is this childlike form of Lord Shiva so deeply worshipped? /  That is the mystery of our next episode.
